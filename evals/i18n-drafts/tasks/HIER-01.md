# Strengthen article h2 hierarchy

Second-level headings in long articles are too easy to miss, so section boundaries do not read clearly.

In the shared article stylesheet, make `h2` headings 2em at normal weight and give later sections 6em of block-start separation. The first section should use a smaller 3em gap. Keep the existing border and uppercase treatment. Ensure print layout does not replace the new section spacing with an obsolete `h2` margin override.

Review a long article on screen and in print preview for hierarchy and excessive whitespace.
